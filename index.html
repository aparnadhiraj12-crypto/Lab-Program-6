About us

We are a small crew of hikers who guide groups along mountain trails, from gentle ridge walks to full alpine days. Every route is chosen for its views and paced so everyone finishes smiling.

Groups never top eight people, so you get room to breathe, stop for photos and ask questions along the way.

What to bring

  • Sturdy hiking boots
  • Layers for changing weather
  • Two litres of water
  • Trekking poles (optional)
